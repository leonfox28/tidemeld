Optional US storefront localization. The app interface remains Simplified Chinese.

## Name

Tidemeld

## Subtitle

Social feeds, together

## Promotional text

Bring your Weibo and X feeds together. Read in time order, switch platforms and manage multiple accounts in a free, open-source reader. Interface in Simplified Chinese.

## Description

Tidemeld brings feeds from multiple social networks into one native reading experience on iPhone and iPad. It is a free, open-source, independent third-party client.

Read your feeds together
• Add Weibo and X accounts, including multiple accounts on the same platform.
• Switch between the combined feed and individual platforms.
• Merge posts in time order and remove duplicate posts within each platform, all on your device.
• See each post's source and explore text, photos, videos, reposts, quotes and comments.

Platform features
• Weibo: search, trending topics, profiles and notifications; likes, comments, replies and reposts; publish text and photos, and delete your own posts and comments.
• X: following feed, posts and comments, with controls for replies, likes, reposts and quotes. X integration is still being improved and differs from Weibo; native search, notifications and standalone new-post publishing are not available for X.

Make reading comfortable
• Light, dark and system appearance.
• Adjustable text size, image quality and image cache limit.

Before you download
• The app interface is in Simplified Chinese. iOS 26 or later is required.
• Without signing in, you can browse some public Weibo posts, trending topics and public web results. Weibo limits guest content and pagination. Combining personal following feeds requires signing in to the corresponding platforms.
• Sign-in takes place on each platform's official website. SMS or other security verification may be required. Session credentials are stored locally for each account.
• Feed merging, sorting and deduplication happen on your device. The maintainer does not operate a backend receiving your platform data; requests go directly to the relevant service.
• Tidemeld is not affiliated with or endorsed by Weibo or X. Content, features and access depend on platform services, your network and account status. Platform interface changes may affect functionality.

# Review notes — version 1.0.0 (11)

Saved to App Store Connect on November 12 for build 11. The submission entered Waiting for Review at 11:10 Asia/Shanghai. Actual status is recorded in [APP_STORE.md](../APP_STORE.md). Existing review contact details remain only in App Store Connect.

---

Tidemeld 1.0.0 (11) is an independent, free, open-source client for Weibo and X on iPhone and iPad. The interface is in Simplified Chinese.

REPORT AND CONTENT FILTER LOCATIONS:
- Report a post: tap the three-dot button (⋯) at the TOP RIGHT of the post, to the right of the platform icon, then choose 举报 (Report).
- Report a comment: open post details, LONG-PRESS the comment, then choose 举报 (Report).
- Content filtering: open the 设置 (Settings) tab → 内容过滤 (Content Filtering). Enter local filter keywords, one per line. This page also explains sensitive-media hiding. X media marked sensitive by the platform is hidden and not loaded by default, with per-item reveal controls. Local filters apply to native content, not platform webpages, and do not classify every unmarked item.

Changes for guideline 1.2:
- Before browsing or connecting any platform account, users must read 使用条款 (Terms of Use), scroll to the bottom, and explicitly tap 同意并继续 (Agree and continue). The terms prohibit objectionable content and abusive behavior. Terms and Privacy Policy remain available offline under 设置 → 关于叠澜 (About).
- Post and profile menus, and comment long-press menus, provide reporting. Weibo opens its official complaint form for the selected item. X opens the original post/profile with instructions to choose Report in the official menu. Users complete and submit the report to the platform; opening the page alone does not submit it.
- The same menus on another user's content offer 拉黑此用户 (Block this user). After confirmation by the source platform, the app immediately hides that user's native content for that source account, including nested quotes and comments. The profile menu provides 取消拉黑 (Unblock).

Guideline 4.8:
We request reconsideration under the exception for clients of specific third-party services requiring users to sign in directly to those services. There is no separate Tidemeld account. Weibo and X are content sources, not alternative identity providers for a primary Tidemeld account. Their sign-ins grant access to the user's existing platform feeds and actions; Sign in with Apple cannot grant those permissions. The picker now says 添加内容来源账号 (Add content-source account) and explains this distinction.

Review access:
- After accepting the terms, guests can browse a limited public Weibo feed, view its media, and open public search pages. Weibo restricts guest browsing; authenticated features require an existing platform account. No test credentials or built-in demo account are supplied.
- To connect an account: 首页 (Home), top-left person icon → 添加账号 (Add Account) → Weibo or X. The official service website opens in WebKit and may require SMS/security verification. The app cannot bypass verification. Please advise if additional review access is needed.
- Session cookies remain per account in the device Keychain. Platform requests go directly to the selected service. There is no developer-operated backend, advertising/analytics SDK, IDFA access or developer telemetry. Official webpages use their own cookies and policies.
- Native X search, notifications and standalone new-post publishing are not implemented. No purchases/subscriptions. Background audio is for user-started playback/Picture in Picture.
- Store screenshots use labeled original sample data generated by offline tests; those samples are not an in-app demo mode.

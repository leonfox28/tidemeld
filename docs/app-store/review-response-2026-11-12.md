Thank you for reviewing Tidemeld.

For guideline 1.2, the revised app requires explicit agreement to the bundled Terms of Use before browsing or connecting a platform account. The terms state that objectionable content and abusive behavior are not tolerated. They remain available under Settings → About → Terms of Use.

Posts, comments and profiles now offer Report in their more-actions menus. Weibo opens its official complaint form with the selected item. X opens the original post or profile with instructions to choose Report in the platform menu and complete its reporting flow. Users submit their reports directly to the respective service; simply opening the page does not submit a report.

Block uses the originating platform account. After the platform confirms the change, Tidemeld immediately hides that user's native content for that account, including nested quotes and comments. Users can unblock from the profile. Existing platform-marked sensitive-media hiding and local keyword filters remain available.

For guideline 4.8, we request reconsideration under the exception for clients of specific third-party services that require users to sign in directly to those services. Tidemeld has no separate Tidemeld user account. Weibo and X are the sources of the content and platform actions, and signing in grants access to each existing platform account. They are not alternative identity providers for a primary Tidemeld account. The account picker now explicitly says “Add content-source account” (添加内容来源账号) and explains this distinction.

Review steps for the revised build:

1. On first launch, read 使用条款 (Terms of Use), scroll to the bottom and select 同意并继续 (Agree and continue).
2. Open the person icon on Home → 添加账号 (Add account). The picker describes the content-source accounts. Connect an existing Weibo or X account through its official website.
3. Open the ⋯ menu on a post or comment, then 举报 (Report), and follow the displayed official platform flow.
4. On content from another user, choose 拉黑此用户 (Block this user) and confirm. Once the platform acknowledges it, the content disappears from the native feed for that source account. The profile menu provides 取消拉黑 (Unblock).
5. Settings → 内容过滤 (Content Filtering) manages local keywords. About provides the offline Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Guest access covers a limited public Weibo feed after terms acceptance. Authenticated actions require a platform account; no test credentials are currently supplied. Please let us know what additional review access is needed and which part of the third-party-service client exception you consider inapplicable.
